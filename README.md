# Exact Simulation of Correlated CEV Processes

Research project investigating the exact simulation of correlated
Constant Elasticity of Variance (CEV) asset-price processes through
squared Bessel (SQB) representations and randomized Gamma transitions.

## Overview

This research report was completed as part of my undergraduate Research Assistant position at Wilfrid Laurier University. The project investigates correlated randomized Gamma variables and their application to the exact simulation of correlated CEV processes through squared Bessel representations.

The project includes:

- Derivation of correlation expressions for randomized Gamma variables
- Common and independent Poisson randomizer structures
- CEV-to-squared-Bessel transformation laws
- Exact simulation with and without absorption
- Gaussian copula dependence
- Lower Fréchet correlation bounds
- Monte Carlo validation of theoretical correlation results
